Our Courses

Courses We Offer

At our polytechnic college, we offer a diverse range of exciting diploma course that empower you to turn your passion into a profession. Whether you’re interested in design engineering,coding, or electronics. Our courses are hands-on, industry-focused, and specifically designed to equip with the skills and expertise needed to succeed in your chosen field and become job ready.

CIVIL

CIVIL Engineering

Civil engineers work as design engineers, project managers, or consultants on infrastructure development projects.They use computer modeling software and data from surveys, site tests, and maps to create project blueprints.

MECH

MECHANICAL Engineering

Mechanical engineering deals with understanding how things work, from the tiniest micro-particle to the largest spacecraft, and even the human body—one of our most complex machines. It is the broadest of all engineering disciplines, and interdisciplinary work is key to our department's success.

AUTO

AUTOMOBILE Engineering

The sound of a great engine, the unity and uniqueness of an automobile's engineering and coachwork,the history of the company and the car, and of course, the sheer beauty of the thing.

ECE

ELECTRONICS & COMMUNICATION Engineering

The Electrical and Communication Engineering field showcase its cutting-edge nature by incorporating innovation concept such as circuits, singals, processing, microchips, and future technologies.

EEE

ELECTRICAL & ELECTRONICS Engineering

Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) department studies and applies the principles of electricity, electronics, and electromagnetism to design develop electrical and electronic devices and systems.

AI & ML

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE and MACHINE LEARNING Engineering

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI&ML) department offers programs that teach students how to develop intelligent systems that learn from data and mimic human behavior.
