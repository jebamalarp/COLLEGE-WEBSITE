Course We Offer

At our college, we offer a diverse range of exciting course that empower you to turn your passion into a profession. Our courses are hands-on, industry-focused, and specifically designed to equip with the skills and expertise needed to succeed in your chosen field and become job ready.

CIVIL

Civil engineers work as design engineers, project managers, or consultants on infrastructure development projects.

MECH

Mechanical engineering deals with understanding how things work, from the tiniest micro-particle to the largest spacecraft, and even the human body—one of our most complex machines.

AUTO

The sound of a great engine, the unity and uniqueness of an automobile's engineering and coachwork,the history of the company and the car, and of course, the sheer beauty of the thing.

ECE

The ECE field showcase its cutting-edge nature by incorporating innovation concept such as circuits, singals, processing, microchips, and future technologies.

EEE

Electrical and Electronics(EEE) department studies and applies the principles of electricity, electronics, and electromagnetism to design develop electrical and electronic devices and systems.

AI & ML

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI&ML) department offers programs that teach students how to develop intelligent systems that learn from data and mimic human behavior.
