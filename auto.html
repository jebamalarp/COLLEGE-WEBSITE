AUTOMOBILE ENGINEERING


Department Overview

The Department Strength is 30 students in each second year and final year..

The Diploma in Automobile Engineering program is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the design, development, manufacturing, and maintenance of automobiles. This program blends theoretical knowledge with practical skills, preparing students for the evolving automotive industry. With a focus on modern technology and sustainable practices, this program enables students to work on advanced vehicle systems, including electric and hybrid vehicles, making them ready for the future of transportation.

Vision

To be a leading center of excellence in automobile engineering, producing industry-ready professionals committed to innovation and sustainability in the automotive sector.


Mission

Equip students with strong technical knowledge in automotive design, manufacturing, and maintenance. Foster innovation and research in advanced vehicle technology. Develop skilled professionals with a focus on safety, sustainability, and quality. Build industry partnerships for hands-on training and internships..
